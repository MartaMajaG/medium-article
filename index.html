The Joy of Kittens

Kittens are nature's tiny chaos agents — impossibly soft, endlessly curious, and completely convinced that 3am is the perfect time for zoomies. From the moment they open their eyes, they approach the world with a boldness that would embarrass most adults. A cardboard box is a fortress. A ball of yarn is a worthy opponent. Your laptop keyboard is, obviously, a bed.

A Kitten's Guide to Choosing Your Human

Not just any human will do. You'll want someone who leaves warm laundry unattended, tolerates being stared at during meals, and understands that "do not disturb" signs do not apply to cats. Bonus points if they own a sunbeam-facing windowsill and keep their laptop open at all times. Once you've identified the right candidate, simply sit on their chest at 6am and the contract is considered signed. 🐾

The Gravity Research Institute (Est. Every Kitchen Counter)

Scientists have long puzzled over why objects fall when pushed from high surfaces. Kittens, however, have been conducting rigorous field research on this very question for centuries. A glass of water on the edge of a table. A pen. Your phone. Each item pushed with one deliberate paw, each result observed with intense scientific detachment. The findings are always the same. The research continues anyway. For science. 🐱
